Two units at the Albizia shopping centre are currently **available to let**,
both of them free immediately.

The first, of **71 m²**, sits in the linear building, between businesses
already in place. It comprises a sales floor of roughly 50 m² at ground level,
with a window looking straight onto the car park, toilets and a 20 m² mezzanine
that can be used for storage or as an office.

The second, of **74 m²**, is a **newly built** floor on the first storey of
the corner building, served by lift and stairs. Its large picture windows give
it plenty of natural light, and the single open floor lends itself to a fit-out
as offices, as a professional practice or as a public reception area.

Both units are accessible to people with reduced mobility and benefit from the
free car park as well as the centre's round-the-clock services — the parcel
locker and the electric charging points.

Full details of each unit are set out in the **Units to let** section. For
rental terms and to arrange a viewing, please contact the agency handling the
letting.
