> **Article unpublished.** The two vending machines announced here (bread and
> prepared meals) have since been removed from the site; only the Bergamo pizza
> machine remains. The article is kept for the record but is no longer listed,
> so as not to advertise a service that no longer exists.

The Albizia shopping centre has installed **two self-service vending machines**,
available **24 hours a day, 7 days a week**.

The first offers **prepared meals** — couscous, paella, sandwiches, salads and
oriental pastries. The second dispenses **bread and pastries**, supplied by the
Fournil de Bressols.

Both machines are freely accessible on site, next to the car park. They meet a
simple need: finding something to eat on a Sunday evening, on a public holiday,
or outside shop opening hours.
