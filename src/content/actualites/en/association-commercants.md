tags:

---

The tradespeople and retailers of the area have come together in the
**« La Mouscane artisans commerçants »** association, chaired at the time by
**Patrick Pillon**, barber at the Albizia shopping centre.

The association brings together the businesses of the centre and those of the
neighbouring building. Its aim: to raise the profile of the area's business
community, build links between the businesses and organise events throughout the
year.

Its first notable initiative was a **fête de la musique** held on 21 June 2023,
with two bands playing and a refreshment stall on site.
