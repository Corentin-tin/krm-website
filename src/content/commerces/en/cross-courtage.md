**Cross Courtage** is the **insurance brokerage** at the Albizia shopping centre
in Montech.

The broker compares the offers available on the market on behalf of its clients
and supports them across all their needs: **home, motor, health and personal
protection insurance** for individuals, and cover for premises, vehicles and
business activity for professionals. The service runs from assessing the need
through to managing the policy.

Clients are received at the centre, where parking is free.
