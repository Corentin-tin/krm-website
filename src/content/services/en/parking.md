The centre has a **free car park** with direct access, supplemented by on-street
parking in the neighbouring roads. No barrier, no parking disc, no time limit:
you park in front of the shop you have come to visit.

The entrance is from the avenue de Montauban (RD 928), at the start of the
Mouscane retail area.
