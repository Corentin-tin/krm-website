An **automated Pickup locker** is installed **inside the Wash'n Dry
launderette**. It takes **Chronopost**, **Colissimo** and **DPD** parcels.

You collect and drop off your parcels using the code you have been sent, without
queuing at a counter. Access follows the **launderette's opening hours**.
