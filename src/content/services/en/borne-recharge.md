Since 2023 the centre has had a **Powerdot electric charging station** offering
**three rapid charging points that can be used at the same time**.

The station is open to all, with no subscription required: payment is made
directly by bank card or through the app. A charge takes long enough to do the
shopping or keep an appointment on site.
